# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX / Product Designers

### Role Summary
UX / Product Designers research user needs and shape clear, accessible experiences in partnership with the Product Manager and Developers.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research or usability testing appropriate to the project
- Create and validate user flows, wireframes, prototypes, and interaction designs
- Document accessibility and experience requirements for acceptance criteria
- Use feedback and product metrics to refine designs during delivery

### Collaboration and Decision Boundaries
- Partner with the Product Manager on user problems and solution options, and with Developers and the Technical Lead on feasibility and implementation details.
- Recommend experience patterns and own design artifacts; the Product Manager remains accountable for product scope and priority, and engineering owns implementation choices.

### Lifecycle Involvement
- Explore user needs during initiation, shape and validate designs during planning and execution, and review usability feedback after release.

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads / Architects guide technical direction so the solution is reliable, maintainable, and consistent with agreed architecture and constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical discovery, design reviews, and architecture decisions
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and non-functional requirements
- Help Developers break down work and agree implementation approaches
- Coordinate technical decisions across teams and document significant trade-offs

### Collaboration and Decision Boundaries
- Work with the Product Manager to assess feasibility and options, and with the Project Manager to surface dependencies and delivery risks.
- Own technical recommendations and decisions within agreed standards and project constraints; changes that affect scope, outcomes, cost, or timeline are agreed with the Product Manager and Project Manager and escalated to stakeholders as needed.

### Lifecycle Involvement
- Assess feasibility during initiation, shape technical plans during planning, guide implementation during execution, and support release readiness and follow-up.

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Leads help teams define measurable outcomes and use trustworthy data to evaluate product and project results.

### Responsibilities
- Advise on success metrics, instrumentation, and data requirements
- Validate event definitions, dashboards, and analysis plans
- Surface data quality, access, and interpretation limitations
- Analyze results and share evidence that can guide iteration

### Collaboration and Decision Boundaries
- Work with the Product Manager and stakeholders to make outcomes measurable, and with Developers and the Technical Lead to plan instrumentation.
- Own analytics methods and data-quality guidance; the Product Manager owns product priorities and outcome decisions, informed by the evidence.

### Lifecycle Involvement
- Define metrics during initiation and planning, validate instrumentation during execution, and analyze outcomes after release.

---

## Security / Privacy Partners

### Role Summary
Security / Privacy Partners help teams identify and address security, privacy, and compliance risks in the solution and its data use.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security, privacy, and data-handling requirements
- Review designs and data flows, and recommend proportionate safeguards
- Help assess risks and track mitigations with the project team
- Advise on required reviews, security scans, and escalation paths

### Collaboration and Decision Boundaries
- Partner with the Product Manager on user and data impacts, with the Technical Lead and Developers on safeguards, and with the Project Manager on risk tracking and escalation.
- Provide guidance and required domain reviews under applicable policies; product and delivery trade-offs remain with the accountable product and project decision-makers. Unresolved risks are escalated through the established governance process before release.

### Lifecycle Involvement
- Identify risks during initiation, review requirements and designs during planning and execution, and support required checks before release.

---

## Operations / Release Owners

### Role Summary
Operations / Release Owners coordinate operational readiness and the execution of planned releases with the delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm deployment steps, ownership, monitoring, and rollback or mitigation plans
- Coordinate release windows and operational dependencies
- Verify operational readiness and post-deployment checks
- Coordinate incident response and communicate operational issues to the team

### Collaboration and Decision Boundaries
- Work with the Project Manager to coordinate timing and communications, with the Technical Lead and Developers on deployment and recovery plans, and with QA/Testing on verification.
- Own operational readiness advice and assigned release execution; scope and release priority remain with the Product Manager, while delivery coordination and stakeholder alignment remain with the Project Manager. Escalate readiness concerns before deployment.

### Lifecycle Involvement
- Identify operational needs during planning, prepare and verify deployment during execution, coordinate release and post-deployment checks, and feed operational learnings into retrospectives.

---

## Customer Support / Success Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support / Success Representatives bring customer feedback into project decisions and prepare customer-facing teams for changes.

### Responsibilities
- Share recurring customer questions, needs, and support trends
- Review proposed changes for supportability and customer impact
- Prepare help content, internal guidance, and support readiness plans
- Relay post-release feedback and emerging issues to the project team

### Collaboration and Decision Boundaries
- Partner with the Product Manager and UX / Product Designer on customer needs, and with the Project Manager on stakeholder updates and readiness activities.
- Advise on customer impact and support readiness; product scope and priority remain with the Product Manager, and release timing is coordinated by the Project Manager with the release owner.

### Lifecycle Involvement
- Contribute customer context during initiation and planning, prepare support materials before release, and share feedback and support trends after release.

---

## Adapting Roles to the Project
- Roles may be combined or adapted to team size, project scope, and context; assign clear ownership for each responsibility even when one person fills multiple roles.
- Involve specialist partners when the project needs their expertise, and agree decision-makers and escalation paths during initiation.

---
